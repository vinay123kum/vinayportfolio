Moving back to India

How long your RNOR window lasts

When you move back, India does not tax you as a full resident straight away. For two or three years you can be Resident but Not Ordinarily Resident — and in that window your foreign income, including your US salary, 401(k) and capital gains, generally stays outside the Indian net. Most people waste it because nobody told them it existed.
