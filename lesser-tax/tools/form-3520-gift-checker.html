Gifts & inheritance

Money from home can cost you $10,000 in penalties

Your parents help with a house deposit. You inherit a share of a flat. None of it is taxable income — the US does not tax gifts in the recipient's hands. But cross $100,000 in a year and you have to report it on Form 3520, and the penalty for staying quiet starts at $10,000 and climbs to a quarter of the gift.

General guidance only, not tax or legal advice. Form 3520 also covers transactions with foreign trusts, which have their own rules not modelled here, and a separate Form 3520-A may be required. The $100,000 threshold applies to gifts from nonresident alien individuals and foreign estates and is set by statute.
