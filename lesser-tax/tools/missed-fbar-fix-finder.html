Missed your FBAR?
Find the right fix.

If you forgot to report your Indian bank accounts in past years, the IRS has two different ways to fix it. They are not interchangeable, and picking the wrong one can cost you penalties the right one would have avoided.

Route A · Late FBAR filing

You reported the income from those accounts on your US returns and only missed the FBAR form. Usually no penalty.

Route B · Streamlined

You also didn't report some of that income. Amended returns, 6 years of FBARs, and often a penalty.
