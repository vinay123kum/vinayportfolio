The brief

A beautiful place that looked ordinary online

Rendezvous is a small resort in the hills above Dharamshala: two cottages, four suites, home-cooked meals and a lot of green. Guests who arrived loved it. The problem was everyone who hadn't arrived yet. There was nothing that showed them what the place felt like.

I designed and built the full site from scratch. Structure, copy, visual direction, room pages and the booking path. Every photo on it is of the real property.

Rooms

Six rooms, six reasons to book

Every room gets its own card, its own photo and a line that says what's special about it. Wildflower has the exposed brick, Bella Vista has the view. Guests pick a room because of a feeling, so each card tries to give one, with a "View Room" button right next to it.

Beyond the room

Selling the stay, not just the bed

Meals, the yoga hall, the library, the trampoline. That's a lot of why people pick Rendezvous over a hotel in McLeodganj. The amenities section puts all of it in one scannable list, next to a photo of the communal dining table.

"We didn't build a hotel. We built an experience." Their words. The site had to be built the same way.

Trust

Real reviews, real map, clear next step

A 3-step booking path (message us, choose your space, arrive), live Google reviews, and a map with directions. These are the three things a first-time guest checks before paying for a place they've never seen.
